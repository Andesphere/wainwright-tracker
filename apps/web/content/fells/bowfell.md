This route climbs Bowfell from the National Trust's Old Dungeon Ghyll car park in Great Langdale. Parking is paid and the car park sits at about 100 m. The Old Dungeon Ghyll Hotel bus stop, about 130 m away, is served by the 516.

The line is 5.1 km long and gains about 800 m. The first 1.3 km are almost level. The route crosses Great Langdale Beck at about 190 m and follows a lane for about 900 m before the path begins.

At about 1.35 km the route joins the path OpenStreetMap names The Band, a long path graded mountain hiking with a grass surface on the map. It climbs for 3 km: about 300 m at 2 km, 520 m at 3 km and 620 m at 4 km. The route passes Three Tarns at about 4.4 km and 720 m, then climbs the last 700 m, mostly graded mountain hiking, to the summit cairn at 902 m.

About 3.9 km of the line follows public footpaths and 3.8 km crosses open access land. Two short sections near the car park, about 100 m in all, are on neither in the data.

Bowfell is 902 m high and belongs to the Southern Fells in Book 4.

The GPX file is the same line, from the car park to the summit. Return the same way, or plan another descent with a map before you set out.
