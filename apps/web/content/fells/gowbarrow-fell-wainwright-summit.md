This route climbs Gowbarrow Fell from the National Trust's Aira Force car park, above the north shore of Ullswater. Parking is paid and the car park sits at about 160 m. The Aira Force bus stop, about 160 m away, is served by the 508, UB1 and UB2.

The line is 2.5 km long and gains about 330 m. The first 600 m follow National Trust permissive paths with a compacted surface, including a flight of steps, and cross Aira Beck at about 365 m. A public footpath then takes the route to just under 1 km, rising to about 220 m.

From there a single path climbs across open access land for about 1.5 km. It passes about 350 m at 1.5 km and 420 m at 2 km. At about 2.4 km the route passes a top that OpenStreetMap names Gowbarrow Fell Summit; the highest point, which the Wainwright list uses, is about 100 m further on at 481 m.

About 475 m of the line follows public footpaths, 2.1 km crosses open access land and about 330 m is on permissive paths only. Only the last few metres are graded on the map, as mountain hiking.

Gowbarrow Fell is 481 m high and belongs to the Eastern Fells in Book 1.

The GPX file is the same line, from the car park to the summit. Return the same way, or plan a longer walk with a map before you set out.
