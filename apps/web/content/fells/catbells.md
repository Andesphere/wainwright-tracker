This route climbs Catbells from a free roadside parking area on the lane below the fell, with room for four or five cars. It sits at about 150 m. The nearest bus stop with a current service is Brandelhow Park, about 380 m away, served by the 77A.

The line is short and steep: 1.3 km with about 300 m of ascent. It begins on a public bridleway for the first 460 m, climbing gently from about 150 m to 200 m. From there the gradient sharpens. Between 0.5 km and 0.9 km the path climbs from about 205 m to 335 m, then runs nearly level for about 100 m before the final pull. The last 350 m rise from about 340 m to the top at 451 m.

Only the final few metres leave the mapped paths. About 1 km of the line follows public rights of way, split between a bridleway and a footpath, and almost all of it crosses open access land.

OpenStreetMap names the top Cat Bells. Catbells is 451 m high, 189th of the 214 Wainwrights by height, and one of the North Western Fells in Book 6.

The GPX file is the same line, from the parking to the summit. Return the same way unless you have planned a longer walk with a map.
