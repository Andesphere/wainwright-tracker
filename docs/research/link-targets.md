# Link targets for wainwrightsbaggers.com

Date: 2026-09-26. Ticket: [#45](https://github.com/Andesphere/wainwright-tracker/issues/45), part of map [#15](https://github.com/Andesphere/wainwright-tracker/issues/15).

Scope: UK Wainwright and Lake District communities, walking sites, clubs, forums, subreddits, Facebook groups, newsletters, podcasts and app directories that could link to or list Wainwrights Baggers. Research only. No one was contacted, nothing was submitted, no accounts were created.

How to read the columns:

- **Waits for app**: the target lists iPhone apps, so it only makes sense once the App Store listing is live (`APP_STORE_LIVE`).
- **Real voice**: the approach only works coming from a named person (Alan as a bagger who leads free walks, or Jorge as the maker), not from a brand.
- **Value** weighs relevance to Wainwright baggers, audience and link type. **Effort** is the work to earn the link.
- Audience numbers are what the target publishes. "Unknown" means nothing public was found.

## Key findings

1. The Wainwright Society is the centre of this niche: a links page, a quarterly magazine that commissions member articles, a member forum, and a public register of about 800 people who finished all 214. Alan is the natural way in.
2. No "best Wainwright apps" comparison exists. Searches return only the App Store pages of competitors (Wainwright Companion, Fellbook, Wainwright Memories, Wainwrights by Doug Colton, Bagged!, Cairns, Peaky Baggers). The general "best hiking apps UK" lists cover OS Maps, AllTrails, Komoot and similar, never a Wainwright tracker. The gap is worth filling with our own honest comparison guide; outreach to general listicles is low odds.
3. The strongest community targets are two podcasts made by and for Lake District walkers (Countrystride, The Wainwright Ramblers). Both need Alan's story, not a pitch.
4. Several sites that look like link targets are competitors: WalkLakes, The Lake District Guide and Hill Bagging all offer their own Wainwright tick list. Hill Bagging still keeps a public "apps for hill walkers" page with a hill-logging category, so it stays on the list.
5. Reddit and Facebook give visitors, not SEO. Their links are nofollow/ugc or not indexed. Reddit rule pages could not be fetched (Reddit blocks automated reads); rules there are unverified and must be read by hand before any post.

## Notes by category

### Clubs and societies

- **Wainwright Society.** Founded 2002 ([home](https://www.wainwright.org.uk/)). The [links page](https://www.wainwright.org.uk/links/) has Organisations, Resources, Photography and Art, Lakeland and Cumbria, and Facebook groups sections. It already lists commercial sites (Walkingworld subscriptions, accommodation, White Lion Publishing), so a useful tool is not out of place. No submission route is published. [Footsteps](https://www.wainwright.org.uk/footsteps-magazine/) runs four issues a year, 6,000 copies a year, about 50 articles a year, the editor commissions member writing, and it carries ads. The [214 register](https://www.wainwright.org.uk/completers/214-completers/) lists about 800 completers with member numbers. Alan finishing his last five fells is a natural register entry and article. The [forum](https://wainwright.proboards.com/) was down (HTTP 503) when checked; ProBoards' general rules treat posts made to promote your own site as advertising ([ProBoards rules](https://support.proboards.com/page/rules)).
- **Ramblers.** The Lake District Area has affiliated groups including Kendal Fellwalkers and Keswick Rambling Club ([Ramblers](https://www.ramblers.org.uk/go-walking/ramblers-groups/areas/lake-district-area)). Reach, not links.
- **Friends of the Lake District.** Offers corporate partnership (volunteering, giving); WalkLakes is a corporate member ([FLD](https://www.friendsofthelakedistrict.org.uk/faqs/walklakes)). Costs money, and no evidence it gives a link. Skip for now.

### Walking and bagging websites

- **Hill Bagging (DoBIH).** Volunteer-run official site for the Database of British and Irish Hills, 20,000+ hills, with its own app ([home](https://www.hill-bagging.co.uk/)). Its [links page](https://www.hill-bagging.co.uk/links/) has a "Mapping apps with hill logging" category and "Other websites for baggers". Contact is a form or contact@hill-bagging.co.uk ([contact](https://www.hill-bagging.co.uk/contact/)). It is also a tracker, but it links out to other logging tools already.
- **wainwrights.info.** David Watson, 2015 to 2026, fell-by-fell guide ([home](https://www.wainwrights.info/)).
- **Paul Beal.** Google My Maps of all 214 plus one per book; links DoBIH and books; active on many social networks ([page](https://www.paulbeal.com/map-of-wainwrights/)).
- **WalkLakes.** Logs Wainwrights, Birketts, Hewitts and others, so it competes with us ([home](https://www.walklakes.co.uk/)). Its [links page](https://www.walklakes.co.uk/lakes_links.html) has no apps.
- **The Lake District Guide.** Own browser-only checklist and a "List your business" entry ([page](https://thelakedistrictguide.com/wainwrights)).
- **Harold Street, Walking Englishman.** One-person sites; small upside ([Harold Street](https://www.haroldstreet.org.uk/wainwrights/), [Walking Englishman](https://www.walkingenglishman.com/walkingthewainwrights.html)).
- **Walkhighlands.** Hosts a Wainwrights list that Much Better Adventures links to; pages returned 403 to automated reads, not verified.

### Podcasts and newsletters

- **Countrystride.** Mark Richards and David Felton; 168 dispatches; contact form invites walk recommendations; Hefted newsletter on Substack; Patreon ([site](https://www.countrystride.co.uk); [CW Herald](https://cwherald.com/news/popular-lake-district-walking-podcast-strides-towards-100th-episode/)).
- **The Wainwright Ramblers.** Lance, Matt and Nick documenting all 214 over five years; 50 episodes; latest 5 Nov 2025 ([Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/podcast/the-wainwright-ramblers/id1574100662)).
- **Visit Lake District Wainwright podcasts.** Eight free audio walks; no external links on the page ([page](https://www.visitlakedistrict.com/things-to-do/outdoor-activities/wainwright-podcasts)). Not a target.

### Magazines

- **Cumbria and Lakeland Walker.** Country Publications Ltd ([site](http://www.cumbriamagazine.co.uk/)). No submissions page found. Pitch is a story, which needs Alan.
- **The Great Outdoors, Outdoors Magic.** Both run "best hiking apps" tests ([TGO](https://www.thegreatoutdoorsmag.com/gear-guides/best-hiking-apps/), [Outdoors Magic](https://outdoorsmagic.com/article/best-hiking-apps/)); TGO returned 403, not verified. Both cover big navigation apps; low odds for a niche tracker.

### App directories and listicles

- **"Best hiking apps UK" lists** checked: [PeakDistrict.org](https://www.peakdistrict.org/best-hiking-apps-uk/) (7 apps: OS Maps, AllTrails, Komoot, Gaia GPS, Outdooractive, Hiiker, OsmAnd; author Alex Hesp-Gollins; email route), [Lake District Hotels top 5](https://www.lakedistricthotels.net/discover/top-5-hiking-apps/) (2022, stale, recommends Hill Lists for Wainwrights), plus the four in row 10. None lists a Wainwright tracker. All need the App Store listing.
- **Competitor Wainwright apps** found: [Wainwright Companion](https://wainwrightcompanion.co.uk/), [Fellbook](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fellbook-track-wainwrights/id6758641864), [Wainwright Memories](https://wainwrightmemories.com/), [Wainwrights by Doug Colton](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/wainwrights/id6443652407), [Bagged!](https://bagged.openair.tools/), [Cairns](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cairns-hillwalking-logbook/id6749090552), [Peaky Baggers](https://apps.apple.com/cd/app/peaky-baggers/id6444408707). No independent page compares them.

### Forums and social

- **UKHillwalking.** Commercial messages (services, products) must be paid Premier Posts, from £5 ([search snippet of UKH forum info](https://www.ukhillwalking.com/forums/info/); page returned 403 to direct fetch).
- **Reddit.** Rule pages blocked to automated reads. Read each subreddit's sidebar by hand before posting.
- **Facebook.** Group rules and member counts need a login. The Wainwright Society links page lists Facebook walking groups, a good starting list.

## Skip or low value

- **Wikipedia** (List of Wainwrights and fell articles): adding our own site is a conflict of interest under Wikipedia's external links guideline. Do not.
- **Mountain rescue teams and LDSAMRA**: listed on WalkLakes' links page; they link to safety resources, not products.
- **Visit Lake District Wainwright podcasts page**: no outbound links.
- **Friends of the Lake District corporate partnership**: paid, no evidence of a link.

No page compares the Wainwright tracking apps. A fair "Wainwright tracking apps compared" guide on our site, written from Alan's use, could rank for "Wainwright app" searches and give listicle writers something to cite. This belongs in the content plan (#21), not in outreach.
